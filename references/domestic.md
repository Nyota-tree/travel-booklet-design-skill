# Domestic trip guidance

Use this when the itinerary stays within the traveler's home country. Adapt to the actual country; "domestic" does not imply China. For a China itinerary, the following are useful decision points, not universal requirements.

## Naming and wayfinding

- Put the name shown on local signage and mapping apps first. Add a shorter explanation in the traveler's preferred language. Include a local-script address, entrance/gate, or map-search string when a street address alone will not identify the correct access point.
- For regions with more than one written or spoken language, include the names a traveler will see on signs; do not invent translations or transliterations.
- Distinguish similarly named railway stations, airports, terminals, scenic-area gates, docks, and hotel branches. An address or entrance photo is often more useful than a wide scenic photo.

## Day-specific logistics

- Intercity rail/flight: show the booked service and times, correct station/terminal, departure-side entrance, security/identity check, boarding display or gate, platform/carriage/seat, luggage handling, and a sensible arrival buffer based on current operator guidance. Gates and platforms can change; tell the traveler which live display to trust.
- Urban travel: write the actual line, direction, transfer, exit, walk, or pickup point when supportable. For ride-hailing, identify the pickup entrance and potential road-side mismatch.
- Rural, mountain, island, or coastal travel: locate the last reliable supplies, restrooms, coverage or offline-map needs, trail/boat alternatives, hard turnaround points, and what to do if a path or service is closed.
- Tickets and reservations: preserve the original voucher/QR in the user's artifact, explain whether an ID, reservation record, or live app is required, and note where on-site rules may change. Do not place real identity numbers or QR codes in reusable examples.
- Practical vocabulary should reflect the place: ticket window, queue, entrance, exit, transfer, luggage storage, rental, rescue, restroom, and local-language signs only where actually relevant.

## Useful content over filler

Add the user's saved food, shopping, and activity notes to the correct stop. If a restaurant or item is only a suggestion, label it as such. For low-density days, use remaining space for verified location, supply, and contingency advice rather than enlarging generic photos.
